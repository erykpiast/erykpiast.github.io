Prices. ฿ to ฿฿฿฿ are Michelin's price bands. "++" means about 18% service and VAT is added. A Bib Gourmand meal is meant to cost no more than about ฿1,000, and most are far cheaper.

Before you go. Hours, menus and prices change. Starred places mostly open for dinner only and book out weeks ahead; many street-food Bibs close by mid-afternoon and take cash only.
